# Consent (read aloud)

> Thank you for helping. I'm testing an app called Recall. It remembers where things are in a room from a scan, and
> tells you where they are when you ask. I'm testing the app, not you: if something is hard, that's the app's fault,
> and it's exactly what I need to find out.
>
> We'll do five short tasks in this room, about an hour in total. I'll time some of them and take notes. Please
> think out loud as you go. You can skip any task, take a break, or stop at any time, with no reason needed.
>
> I'll walk the path with you first, and I'll stay close the whole time. If anything feels unsafe, say "stop".
>
> Your name won't be published. My notes stay on my computer; only times and scores, without your name, go into
> the project.

**Question 1:** Are you happy to take part? **Yes / No**

> Separately: I'm making a short video about the app for a competition. I'd like to use clips of you using it, or
> things you say. That's completely optional, and saying no changes nothing about today.

**Question 2:** May I record video or audio and use clips or quotes in the public demo video? **Yes / No**
(If yes: may your face be shown? **Yes / No**)

Record both answers, the date and the time in the results file. If either answer later changes, delete what
it covered.
