# Questions at the end (spoken)

For each statement: "On a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is strongly disagree and 5 is strongly agree…"

1. Asking where something is was easy.
2. The answers told me what I needed: where it is and how high.
3. I trusted the directions enough to walk with them.
4. I understand what the app keeps and what it sends away.
5. I would use this at home.

Then, open questions (write down their words):

6. What was the hardest part?
7. If you could change one thing first, what would it be?
